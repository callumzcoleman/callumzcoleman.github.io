About

I’m an experienced public affairs professional with a track record of influencing government policy in highly-regulated sectors. My background and expertise is in tech, energy, transport, and innovation policy.

When I’m not working I run and write — mostly about technology, running, and the occasional detour into music and history. You can read those thoughts on my newsletter.

I’m also a runner and race director. This is the first website I’ve built with the help of AI — please blame your chatbot of choice for any particularly egregious shortcomings.

Ten things

Ten things I’m proud of from the past year.

  1. Completing Border to Hel, a 480km race through rural Poland. My team finished second and I ran 104km at ~3:40/km pace.
  2. Successfully influencing government regulation of heat networks, securing better legal protections for c.20% of UK consumers by 2040.
  3. Winning the Oxford Town & Gown 10k, a closed-road race with 7,000 participants that runs through the centre of the city I call home.
  4. Getting a new job, leading public affairs on a key strategic project for the University of Oxford.
  5. Building a tight-knit community in a new city after a stint of travelling abroad.
  6. Using a two-week gap between jobs to undertake a solo, self-planned hut-to-hut running adventure through the Julian Alps in Slovenia.
  7. Starting a Substack on tech policy, music, and running.
  8. Running a shiny new 5k PB (15:15) after a difficult period of illness.
  9. Upskilling in AI policy via a series of intensive courses and reading, as I think it’s the most pressing issue I can work on.
  10. Gifting my Dad a holiday abroad for Christmas — his first trip out of the UK in several years.
